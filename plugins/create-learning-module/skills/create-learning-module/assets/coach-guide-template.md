**Participant guide:** `<module>.participant.guide.md`  
**Purpose:** Help the learner surface and apply the module's key learning points without reteaching it.

## Coach the journey

### 1. Scenario

- Ask what stood out to the participant.
- Listen for their first instinct, choice, or observation.
- Offer one small nudge only when they are stuck.

### 2. Exercise

- Check that the participant follows the steps and notices what changes.
- Ask them to explain the core idea in their own words.
- Listen for the key concepts and important boundaries.

### 3. Reflection

1. Ask for one specific example from the participant's work.
2. Ask where the core idea could change their choice or approach.
3. Offer one concise nudge only if the connection remains abstract.

### 4. Takeaways and next step

- [ ] The participant can explain their key takeaways.
- [ ] The output meets the participant guide's criteria.
- [ ] The participant has named one specific action for tomorrow.

Point to the participant guide rather than copying its explanations, instructions, exercises, or answers.
