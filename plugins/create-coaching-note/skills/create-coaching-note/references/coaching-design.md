# Coaching-note design

## Purpose

A coaching companion helps a person or team apply one atomic idea in context. It is not a lesson transcript, participant workbook, rollout plan, compliance policy, or duplicate of the source note.

The atomic note explains the knowledge. The coaching companion adds prompts, practices, signals, and support.

## Shape the guidance

### Coaching intent

State the change in attention, conversation, or behavior the coaching should enable. Keep the learner or team as the actor.

### Consider

Name enabling conditions or tensions worth considering. Useful considerations may include psychological safety, local constraints, incentives, accessibility, or who bears the cost of change.

### Start, continue, stop

- **Start:** introduce one small, testable practice.
- **Continue:** preserve something already helping.
- **Stop:** remove a behavior that undermines the idea.

Avoid universal mandates and detailed implementation steps. The note should guide judgment, not replace it.

### Questions

Use open questions that invite a concrete example, trade-off, or next experiment. Avoid questions with an obvious approved answer.

### Signals and metrics

Choose observable signals that help people learn whether the practice is useful. Prefer a balanced set such as reuse, quality, sentiment, lead time, participation, or an explicit qualitative check.

State how a signal could be gamed or misread when that risk matters.

### Resistance and support

Treat resistance as information. Explore workload, trust, autonomy, unclear value, or prior experience before escalating. Prefer support such as examples, recognition, peer learning, smaller experiments, or removing friction.

Do not recommend punishment, public shaming, forced participation, or metrics used primarily for surveillance.

## Preserve graph integrity

The coaching note:

- uses the same `Parent` MOC as the atomic note;
- uses `Companion to: [<atomic-note-title>](<atomic-stem>.md)`;
- includes a domain tag, `#coaching`, one workflow tag, and one visibility tag;
- may include meaningful internal links, but never a decorative related-links list;
- defaults to portable relative Markdown links while allowing wiki-style links for PKM tools that prefer them.

## Final review

Confirm that the coaching note:

- supports rather than restates the atomic idea;
- gives room for context and learner choice;
- includes a useful start, continue, and stop;
- uses metrics for feedback rather than control;
- anticipates resistance without blaming people;
- contains no unsupported claims or invented evidence.
