# Coaching-companion design

## Ground the companion

Use the approved atomic note as the complete knowledge boundary. Preserve its pattern, mechanism, signals, learning, and constraints. Do not reopen original sources or convert uncertainty into certainty.

The atomic note explains reusable knowledge. The companion turns that knowledge into a supportive coaching experience. It is not a lesson transcript, participant workbook, rollout plan, compliance policy, or duplicate explanation.

## Design for discovery and experience

Explain the pattern in language appropriate to the named audience. Correct a meaningful misconception only when the atomic note supports the correction.

Use observable situations to help a coach recognize relevance without labelling motives or diagnosing people. Questions should invite concrete examples, trade-offs, and reflection rather than lead toward an approved answer.

Choose an exercise that lets the learner experience the pattern at low cost. Its steps should be feasible in the coaching context, and its expected outcome should describe what the learner will produce, notice, or be able to discuss—not promise a business result.

## Support voluntary adoption

Balance three kinds of action:

- introduce one small behaviour;
- preserve an existing behaviour that already helps;
- remove or reduce a behaviour that works against the pattern.

Keep each action proportionate and revisable. Avoid universal mandates and detailed implementation plans.

Use progress signals for feedback and learning. Balance observable evidence with context, and state what the signals cannot establish. Do not turn measures into quotas, surveillance, or proxies for individual worth.

Treat resistance as information about workload, trust, autonomy, accessibility, unclear value, incentives, or prior experience. Respond by exploring the concern and reducing friction. Never recommend punishment, public shaming, or forced participation.

## Preserve graph integrity

The coaching companion:

- uses the atomic note's one parent MOC;
- links back to the atomic note;
- includes the atomic note's domain tags, `#coaching`, one workflow tag, and one visibility tag;
- may use meaningful internal links but never a decorative related-links list;
- defaults to portable relative Markdown links while allowing wiki-style links for compatible PKM tools.

## Review

Confirm that the companion:

- teaches rather than copies the atomic pattern;
- helps the coach recognize relevance without diagnosing people;
- uses open questions and a feasible experiential exercise;
- leaves room for learner context and choice;
- treats progress signals as evidence for reflection, not control;
- responds to resistance without blame or coercion;
- contains no unsupported recommendations or invented evidence.
