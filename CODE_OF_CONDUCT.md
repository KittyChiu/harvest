# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and inclusive environment
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Welcome questions and differing viewpoints.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's information without consent.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
and other abusive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer using the contact details on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/KittyChiu). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
